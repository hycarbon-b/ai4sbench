Review a proposal

Only approved reviewers can publish a human review. First apply for reviewer access, then sign in with your approved GitHub identity.

  1. Open the task board and select a proposal.
  2. Read the proposal, its Discussion, prior human reviews, and any AI recommendation. Check scientific value, feasibility, reproducibility, and whether the proposed outcome can be evaluated.
  3. In the reviewer workbench on the task detail page, choose Approved, Changes requested, or Rejected. Fill in the structured assessment and actionable comments.
  4. Preview your review and publish it. The site adds a new review reply to the GitHub Discussion and keeps it in the review history. A later review adds another entry rather than replacing the earlier one.

AI review is advisory and does not change the human decision. A contributor may open a PR for a new task only after human approval. For that handoff, share the approved Discussion URL and the task guide.
